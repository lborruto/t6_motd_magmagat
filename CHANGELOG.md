# Changelog

## 1.0.1 - 2026-10-02

The Magmagat now matches Black Ops 4's own script more closely:

- Zombies on fire burn for 8 seconds at most (was 12), as in BO4.
- Two lava pools at once at most (was three): a new one puts out the oldest.
- Brutus caught in a pool or a burst is scorched once and burns for 8 seconds, without further damage.
- A blob that sticks to a teammate drops to the floor under him and makes its pool there, instead of vanishing.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-02

The first release: a new wonder weapon and its quest for Black Ops II Zombies, Mob of the Dead, on Plutonium. One
mod folder, `zm_magmagat`: pick it in **Mods**, then play Mob of the Dead.

- **The Magmagat quest from the BO3 remaster.** The quest follows the BO3 Workshop map "MOB OF THE DEAD" by
  copforthat, step by step: the fireplace in the Warden's Office, its lockdown, the souls, the run with the tempered
  gun and the forge. It opens once the plane has flown, for any player holding a Blundergat, a Sweeper, an Acid Gat
  or a Vitriolic Withering.
- **BO4-style essences and the deposit.** Zombies that die in the locked-down office drop an essence you walk over;
  it streaks into a skull on the mantle, and the three skulls light up as the souls come in. Once they are full, you
  deposit the essence in the fire before you can take the Tempered Blundergat.
- **The tempered gun and the forge.** The tempered gun burns blue and burns out: blue drums along the route keep it
  alive on the way to the Generator Room. Only a gun still burning goes on the forge; the press works it into the
  Magmagat, and taking it calls a Brutus. A Pack-a-Punched gun comes out as the Magmus Operandi, and every player can
  forge his own.
- **The Black Ops 4 Magmagat.** BO4's own model on the Blundergat's animations, and BO4's own rules: a lobbed lava
  blob that sticks, blows its zombie apart (or burns a tough one down), bursts to set the ones around on fire, and lays
  a short lava pool where it misses that sets alight whoever walks in and draws a few zombies. Pack-a-Punch it for the
  Magmus Operandi, in Mob's Pack-a-Punch camo. The Acid Gat kit turns it back into an Acid Gat, and the fireplace
  takes the Acid Gat again, never lowering its tier.
- **The forge's ghosts.** Two Afterlife ghosts rise out of the floor to work the press, and vanish as the Magmagat
  comes out.
- **The remaster's look and sound.** The remaster's own effects (the blue flames, the souls, the lockdown, the press
  fire, the lava blob's trail and burst, the lava pool, the muzzle flashes), its dark-green drums and mantle
  skulls, and its sounds (the flame bursts, the souls, the press, the warden's lines).

The full walkthrough, behind spoiler folds, is in [docs/GUIDE.md](docs/GUIDE.md).
