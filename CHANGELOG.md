# Changelog

## 1.0.4 - unreleased

The Magmagat, 1:1 with Black Ops 4's own script:

- One blob kills Brutus in a few seconds in early rounds; a lava pool burns him hard.
- Every hit pays points, and Insta-Kill works with the Magmagat.
- A lava pool lures 3 zombies (the Magmus Operandi 6), not every zombie near it.
- Twelve enemies burn at most at once.
- A zombie with a blob bursts only when the Magmagat kills it, tearing limbs off its neighbours; a second blob hurts it
  again, and a tough one slows to 60 % of its speed.
- The Acid Gat kit keeps the Magmagat's ammo, and the Mystery Box offers no Blundergat while you hold a Magmagat.
- Chains on the forge machine's feet.

## 1.0.3 - 2026-10-05

The forge and the quest's props, closer to Black Ops 4:

- The forge plays Black Ops 4's scene: two ghouls rise out of the bed, pull the press's lever and fly off through the
  roof while the press works the gun in fire and sparks. The Magmagat then floats over the bed until you take it.
- The forge has moved slightly, and stays closed until you power the Machine.
- The mantle skulls are Black Ops 4's: each turns into the Afterlife skull as it fills.
- The temper drums are filled with ash and burnt wood.
- The fireplace wakes in an orange burst of flame.
- The Magmagat and the Magmus Operandi use Black Ops 4's specular and gloss maps; the Magmus Operandi is brighter.

## 1.0.2 - 2026-10-04

- Standing in your own lava pool burns your screen again.
- The weapon uses Black Ops 4's enemy classes (zombies, fodder, bosses). On Mob of the Dead, Brutus is the boss, as
  before.

## 1.0.1 - 2026-10-02

Closer to Black Ops 4's own script:

- Burning zombies burn for 8 seconds at most (was 12).
- Two lava pools at once at most (was three): a new one removes the oldest.
- Brutus caught in a pool or a burst is scorched once, without further damage.
- A blob that sticks to a teammate drops to the floor and makes its pool there.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-02

The first release: the Magmagat and its quest for Mob of the Dead, on Plutonium.

- **The quest** from copforthat's BO3 remaster: the fireplace in the Warden's Office, the lockdown, the souls, the run
  with the tempered gun and the forge. It opens once the plane has flown, for anyone holding a Blundergat, Sweeper,
  Acid Gat or Vitriolic Withering.
- **BO4-style essences and the deposit**: souls drop as essences you walk over, and you deposit them in the fire
  before taking the Tempered Blundergat.
- **The Black Ops 4 Magmagat**: BO4's model and rules. Pack-a-Punch gives the Magmus Operandi; the Acid Gat kit turns
  it back into an Acid Gat.

The full walkthrough is in [docs/GUIDE.md](docs/GUIDE.md).
